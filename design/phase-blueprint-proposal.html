My Working Direction

Lotte Jansen · finished 2 October 2026

My life picture in three sentences

A slower week by the sea, with the children outdoors after school. Work that pays enough and ends at six. A house we can afford without both of us working weekends.

The direction I want to explore

The Silver Coast, renting for a trial year, both of us working remotely.

Why: two or three reasons

Our ordinary days there look like the life we described. The money lasts long enough to be wrong once. The children are curious rather than against it.

My must-haves

A school the children can walk to. Fast internet. A hospital within 30 minutes.

My dealbreakers

Being further than four hours from my parents. Giving up my own income.

Step 2 · Explore

My Explore Summary

Lotte Jansen · finished 28 October 2026

The place I want to explore further

São Martinho do Porto, Leiria district — the small town, not the village.

Three reasons it fits my picture

A school within walking distance. Fibre at the address we looked at. The hospital in Caldas da Rainha is 20 minutes away.

What a normal month costs there

€2,450, checked against rentals, the energy tariff and a real supermarket list. October 2026.

What it does not give me, and how I deal with that

It is quiet out of season. We will visit in February before we sign anything.

What is still unknown

Whether the school has places for both children. What the winter damp is like in a house of that age.
