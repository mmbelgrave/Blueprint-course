# Bringing the app in line with the repositioning

19 October 2026. The source is "The Life You Choose: Repositioning Summary"
and the three new workbooks. The app's content was built from older
revisions, so it is behind on all three steps.

| Step | App was built from | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Step 1 Picture | Rev.01b | Rev.01e |
| Step 2 Explore | Rev.01a | Rev.01c |
| Step 3 Decide | "Step 3.docx" | Rev.01b |
| Introduction | Rev.01e | Rev.01g |

The summary's staying-route gate is the measure. The broad public promise
goes live only when a person who intends to stay can start without pretending
to consider emigration, skip the country questions without losing the route,
finish with a concrete change plan, and recognise that result in the app and
the download.

## What changed, step by step

**Step 1 Picture (Rev.01e)**

1. The vocabulary. "My new life" becomes "the life I want". The money columns
   "In my new life" become "After the change". This runs through every page,
   every table column and the result document.
2. New: a clarity score before Part 1 (1 to 10, and what makes it that
   number) and again at the end of Part 5, scored without looking back.
3. New: "What I am responsible for: people I care for, work, money" in
   Part 2, which is the gate's "the picture holds aspiration and
   responsibilities".
4. 5.1 merges "Assumptions I still need to check" and "What I need to learn
   next" into one field, "Questions to settle".
5. Part 4 is reframed: "Moving is one option. Changing your work, your time
   or your home where you are is another."
6. 1.2 now names the free Ordinary Tuesday PDF and invites people to bring
   their answer.
7. Mwata's stories are corrected in five places (dates, the programme name
   "Your Roadmap 2022", a new story in Part 4).

**Step 2 Explore (Rev.01c)** — the biggest piece.

1. A whole staying path, S1 to S7: describe the change, the money check at
   the changed income, the rules at work or at home, time after the change,
   the people involved, test your change in real life, and My Explore
   Summary (staying).
2. Parts 1 and 2 become optional, with "Exploring a change where you live?"
   notes on each, and Parts 3 to 5 are skipped on the staying path.
3. A new section, "Leaning towards staying?", for somebody who reaches Part 5
   and finds the life they want does not need another country.
4. A second result: My Explore Summary (staying).

**Step 3 Decide (Rev.01b)**

1. The decision is about "a move or a change where you live" throughout.
2. The staying part gains: what I keep, the small test I run first, money and
   time after the change, and what is still uncertain.
3. A completed example (Rosa), who tested her change in Step 2.

**Introduction (Rev.01g)** — small, to be read in full.

## The order of work

1. Step 1: the vocabulary, the two clarity scores, responsibilities, the 5.1
   merge, the stories.
2. Step 2: the staying path S1 to S7, the routing notes, My Explore Summary
   (staying).
3. Step 3: the decision wording and the four new fields in the staying part.
4. The Introduction and the free Ordinary Tuesday exercise.
5. The app's own words, which are not in any workbook: tab labels, the AI
   partner's prompts, the emails, the result documents. The gate says they
   must not add travel, visas or a foreign home on their own.
6. The result documents: My Explore Summary (staying) has to exist as a
   printed document, like My Change Plan does.
7. A read of the live website against all of it.

## The rule about field ids

Ids are the keys saved answers hang on, so they do not change when only the
wording does. Where the workbook genuinely merges two questions into one
(5.1), the surviving field keeps the older id and the other is retired. Three
accounts exist and none of them is a paying customer, so nothing of value is
at risk either way; this is about the next hundred.

## What is already done

Sample B, the full fictional staying example the gate asks for, exists and is
printed from the app: `design/results/sample-b/`. It was built against the
app as it is today, so it will need a second pass once Steps 1 to 3 carry the
new wording.
