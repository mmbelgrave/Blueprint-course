# Wording for the lawyer: the app notice, the AI consent box, and withdrawal

Drafted 18 October 2026 for Belgrave Management, Unipessoal Lda. Nothing here
is published. It is the text the app will carry once approved, and the one
change the website terms need once the withdrawal button exists.

It follows the pre-review of 17 October and the website pages as published on
18 October, so the app says the same thing as the website rather than a second
version of it. Where the review and the published site differ, the published
site wins, because that is what people have read.

**What the owner has decided**, so the lawyer is not asked to choose:

1. AI consent is asked once, in the app, before the first AI chat. The box at
   sign-up goes. The separate "Mwata may read my answers" box stays in
   Settings, unticked.
2. The three accounts that consented under the old sign-up wording are asked
   again.
3. On withdrawal, access ends three working days after the request, not at
   once and not when the money arrives. The three days are a grace period for
   somebody who changes their mind.
4. Refunds: the full price in both windows, which is option A of the review and
   what the published terms already say.

**The three questions still open for the lawyer** are listed at the end.

## 1. The app's privacy notice, in full

This replaces the page at app.maderealblueprint.com/privacy. It is a summary
that ends in a link to the full note on the website, so the two can never
drift apart again.

### Your privacy

Here is exactly what happens with what you write in this app.

#### Who is responsible

The Life You Choose is a brand of **Belgrave Management, Unipessoal Lda**, a
company registered in Portugal, tax number (NIPC) 518428338. The company is
responsible for your personal data, which in legal terms makes it the
"controller". Mwata runs it. Questions about your data:
info@maderealblueprint.com.

The full privacy note, with everything below in more detail, is at
maderealblueprint.com/privacy.html. This app is for adults, 18 and over.

#### What we store, and why

- Your email address, your first name and your sign-in code, to give you
  access. We do this to deliver what you bought (our contract with you).
- Your answers, your pictures, your feedback and your settings, to run the app
  and remember where you are. Our contract with you.
- Your chats with your AI partner, and the short notes it keeps about your
  answers, so it can help you think. Your consent, which you give by ticking
  the box before your first chat and can withdraw in Settings at any time.
- A log of how much you use your AI partner, to keep the daily limit and to
  follow what it costs. Our legitimate interest in running the app sustainably.
- A question you send from Help, to answer it. Our contract with you.
- Your order, kept as long as tax law requires. Our legal obligation.

#### Who reads it

Your AI partner reads your answers only if you have switched it on. Mwata can
read your answers and the AI notes only if you tick the separate box in
Settings. Both are optional, both can be changed at any time, and with both off
nobody reads what you write but you.

#### Sensitive things

The exercises ask about your life, so write honestly. Please avoid special
categories of data, for example health, religion, sexual orientation or
political opinions, unless you want your AI partner to take them into account.
If you choose to write them, you give your explicit consent to their processing
as described here, by ticking the box shown before your first chat. You can
withdraw that consent in Settings, and you can delete everything the AI partner
knows under "What my AI partner knows".

#### Your pictures

The pictures you add in 1.2 are kept in your own folder, which only you can
open. Nobody can reach them with a plain link. Your AI partner never sees a
picture: it reads only the line you write under it.

#### What is stored in your browser

The app keeps three things in your browser: a sign-in session, so you stay
signed in; your light or dark choice; and the exercise you last opened, so you
can pick up where you stopped. None of them is used for advertising or
tracking.

#### The help we use

Five companies help run this app. **Supabase** stores your account and your
answers in a database in the European Union. **Anthropic** runs the AI model
that your AI partner uses: it is in the United States, it does not use what you
write to train its models, and it deletes what it receives within 30 days,
unless the law requires otherwise or a message is flagged for misuse.
**Vercel** runs the app itself, from the United States. **Resend** delivers the
emails: your sign-in code, and the questions you send from Help. **Lemon
Squeezy** takes the payment if you buy, and holds the order.

Where data goes to the United States it is protected by the EU-US Data Privacy
Framework, by the standard contractual clauses approved by the European
Commission, or by both. The complete list of services and safeguards is in the
full note.

#### How long

As long as you use the Blueprint. If you have not signed in for **24 months**,
we email you first; if there is no reply within 30 days, the account and
everything in it are deleted. Sign-in codes last an hour. The log of how much
you used your AI partner is kept for 12 months, and questions you send from
Help for 12 months after we last spoke. An order has to be kept as long as tax
law says, which is generally 10 years, so after you delete your account the
order record is all that is left. A withdrawal request is kept with the order
record, for the same reason. Backups may hold deleted data for up to 7 days.

#### Deleting

Go to **What my AI partner knows** and choose **Delete everything**. Your
answers, your pictures, your chats, the notes, your questions and your account
are gone at once, apart from the order record and the backups above. You can
also make your AI partner forget one single topic and keep the rest.

#### Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it,
to limit how we use it, to object to it, or to give you a copy in a common file
format. You can withdraw a consent at any time without giving a reason. Write
to info@maderealblueprint.com and we answer within one month.

If you are not happy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You
also have the right to complain to the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados
(CNPD), www.cnpd.pt, or to the data protection authority in the EU country
where you live.

## 2. The AI consent box, shown before the first chat

Shown inside the AI panel the first time somebody opens it, and again if this
wording changes. Until the box is ticked and the button pressed, nothing a
person writes leaves the app.

> **Your AI partner is optional**
>
> If you switch it on, what you write in this chat, your answers on this page
> and your earlier answers are sent to Anthropic, the company whose AI model it
> uses. Anthropic is in the United States. It does not use what you write to
> train its models, and it deletes what it receives within 30 days, unless the
> law requires otherwise or a message is flagged for misuse. Your AI partner
> also keeps short notes about your answers, which you can read and delete
> under "What my AI partner knows".
>
> Please avoid special categories of data, for example health, religion,
> sexual orientation or political opinions, unless you want your AI partner to
> take them into account.
>
> ☐ I agree that my AI partner may read what I write and send it to Anthropic,
> a company in the United States, so it can reply. (needed for your AI partner)
>
> ☐ I also agree that it may read special categories of data if I choose to
> write them, for example about my health, my beliefs, my sexual orientation or
> my political opinions. (optional)
>
> [Switch on my AI partner] [Not now]
>
> Optional. You can switch it off again in Settings at any time, and everything
> else in the app keeps working.

Both boxes are empty when the screen opens, and the button stays dead until
the first one is ticked. The second box is separate on the lawyer's
instruction of 18 October: the AI partner runs on the first box alone, and
special categories are their own explicit consent.

Each answer is written to a `consents` table that is append-only: the user id,
the minute in UTC, the version of this wording, and the value of each box.
Switching a tick off in Settings adds a row saying so rather than changing the
row that recorded the tick. Changing a word here bumps the version, and
everybody is asked again. No IP address is kept.

**Two lines always visible in the panel:** "You are talking to an AI, not a
person." under the name, and "Please avoid health details and other sensitive
data unless you want your AI partner to use them." under the chat box.

**The line at the top of the panel, always visible:** You are talking to an AI,
not a person.

**In Settings**, the same wording appears when somebody switches the AI partner
on from there, and switching it off reads: Your AI partner is off. What you
have already written stays until you delete it under "What my AI partner
knows".

## 4. What the lawyer is asked to confirm

1. **Is the online withdrawal function required for this product?** The
   requirement we found sits in Directive 2023/2673, which covers distance
   contracts for financial services. For digital content, Directive 2011/83
   appears to require the information, the 14 days and a model form, which the
   published terms already give. We are building the button anyway. The
   question is whether it is a duty with a deadline, and whether Portugal's
   transposition prescribes a label or wording for it.
2. **Is "access ends three working days after the request" acceptable?** The
   refund is unaffected and always the full price. The three days are a grace
   period, not a condition.
3. **The explicit consent wording in section 2**, against Article 9(2)(a): is
   one box covering both the processing and the special categories enough, or
   should the special-category sentence have its own box?

Two smaller ones, carried over from the pre-review and not yet closed: the
conformity period in Terms §12 under Decreto-Lei 84/2021, and which consumer
dispute entity is competent for this sector and district.
