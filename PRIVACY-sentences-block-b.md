## 1. Sensitive details (item B1)

**Where:** the section "Please keep the sensitive things out", replacing the
sentence that says the consent is given "through the second box shown before
your first AI chat".

> The exercises ask about your life, so write honestly. Please avoid details
> about health, religion, sexual orientation or political opinions, unless you
> want them in your answers. If you choose to write them, the app stores them
> with your answers. We ask you once, on your first screen, whether you agree
> to that, and you can give or take back that agreement at any time in
> Settings. It is optional, and the app works the same without it. If you
> switch your AI partner on, a second, separate box asks whether such details
> may also go to the AI model. Both are your explicit consent
> (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). The app does not check what you type.

**True today:** the box is on the first screen after the first sign-in, empty,
with the lawyer's three sentences. The answer is written to the consent table
as kind `sensitive` with its own wording version (2026-10-24), including a no.
The box is also in Settings, where it can be given or taken back. Nothing in
the app behaves differently either way.

## 2. Your AI partner and the pages you answer together (item B2)

**Where:** the section "Your AI partner", as a new paragraph at the end.

> If you share the course with a partner, some pages are answered together.
> Your AI partner only reads those pages, and only keeps notes about them, if
> both of you have switched your AI partner on. Until then it stays out of
> them, and your own pages are not affected. A note made from a page you
> answered together is shown to both of you, and either of you can delete it,
> which deletes it for both. Each of you gives your own agreement; neither of
> you can give it for the other.

**True today:** every AI request is refused on a shared page unless both
people have a current, granted AI consent, and the shared answers are left
out of everything that is sent and out of the notes that are kept. Five of
the fifteen notes are drawn from shared pages: your time, your money picture,
your options, the places you are exploring, and the patterns note. Those five
carry the line "Your partner can see and delete this note too", and deleting
one empties it in both accounts.

## 3. Chats, and switching the AI partner off (item B3)

**Where:** the section "How long", after the sentence about the usage log.

> Your chats with your AI partner are kept for 12 months from the day you
> write them, then deleted automatically. The notes your AI partner keeps
> stay until you delete them. If you switch your AI partner off, we ask you
> whether to delete your chats and notes at once or keep them switched off. If
> you keep them, they are deleted 12 months after you switched it off.

**True today:** a daily routine deletes chat lines older than 12 months and
writes one line in the admin log saying what it did. Switching the AI partner
off in Settings asks the question and offers "Delete my chats and notes now".

**True today:** all of it. The letter, the 30-day deletion and the expiry of

## One question for the lawyer

A partner's account follows the same quiet-account rule as anybody else's: 24
months without signing in, a letter, a reminder, and deletion 30 days later.
The buyer's three years of access do not protect the partner's account,
because the three years are the buyer's purchase. So a partner who never signs
in can lose their account while the seat is still open. That seems right to
us, and the seat simply closes, but it is the one place where the two rules
meet and it is worth a second opinion.
